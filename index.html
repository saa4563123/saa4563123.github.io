About Me

I am a B.S.–M.S. combined program student in Electronic Engineering at Korea National University of Transportation, working in the Soft Electronics & Robotics Lab. under Prof. Da Wan Kim. My interests lie in biomimetic robots, robot control systems, and quadruped robots, together with smart materials such as shape-memory polymers and silicones. I am currently conducting research on biomimetic robots and quadruped robots, building on hands-on experience in low-level robot control with ROS 2.
